Privacy Policy for SimplePhoneDirectory

Last updated: October 19, 2026

SimplePhoneDirectory (“the app”) is provided by Yufim (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Contact Information

The app stores the names and phone numbers you enter in its private storage on your device. It does not read your device’s system address book.

We do not operate a server that receives your saved contacts, and the app does not send those contacts to advertising services.

When you choose to call a contact, the app passes the selected phone number to your device’s dialler.

2. Optional Google Drive Backup

You may connect a Google account to manually back up and restore your contacts.

When you request a backup, the app sends your saved contact names, phone numbers, and internal contact identifiers to a private application-data area in your Google Drive account. This data is not normally visible in your regular Drive file list.

The app requests access to its own application-data area, rather than general access to your Drive files. It stores the selected account’s email address and backup status on your device to support this feature.

Connecting an account does not start scheduled backups. Backup and restoration are initiated by you.

3. Android Backup and Exported Files

Separately from the app’s manual Drive backup feature, Android may back up or transfer the app’s locally stored contacts and language preference, depending on your device settings and the backup service available on your device.

If you export contacts to a file, that file contains personal information. Its storage and sharing depend on the location or service you select. Please keep exported files secure.

4. Advertising

The app displays banner advertisements using Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK.

The advertising SDK collects and shares information such as IP addresses, device and account identifiers, app and advertisement interactions, and diagnostic information. IP addresses may be used to estimate approximate location. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Your saved contact names and phone numbers are not included in the app’s advertising requests.

Where applicable, the app requests advertising consent. You can revisit available choices through the app’s “Ad privacy” option when displayed. Android also provides advertising ID controls in device settings.

For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s advertising SDK data disclosures.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Contacts remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app’s local data. Uninstalling the app removes its local data, but previously created cloud backups and exported files may remain.

Deleting a contact locally does not remove it from older backups. Each manual Drive backup creates a separate snapshot. Disconnecting your Google account in the app does not delete existing Drive backups or revoke access granted through Google.

To remove cloud backups, use the relevant Google Drive application-management or device-backup controls. You can also review and revoke the app’s access through your Google account settings. Revoking access alone does not necessarily delete stored backups.

Exported files must be deleted from the locations where you saved or shared them. Google handles retention of advertising-related information under its own policies.

6. Security

The app stores local contacts in Android’s private application storage and uses HTTPS to communicate with Google Drive. Google’s advertising SDK encrypts its data in transit.

No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Please protect your device, Google account, and exported backup files.

7. Support Enquiries

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you include. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and retain it only as needed to handle the issue or meet applicable legal obligations.

Please avoid sending your contact list or other unnecessary personal information. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention requirements.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
